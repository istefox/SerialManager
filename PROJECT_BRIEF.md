## What we are building

A personal macOS app to record the serial numbers and license keys of the software the owner has bought.
Each entry describes one product license (product name, vendor, serial or license key, registered email,
purchase date, version and notes), and the app lets the user add, edit, search and copy these entries.

## Why

License keys end up scattered across emails, PDFs and notes. A single, searchable place makes
reinstalling software or moving to a new Mac quick and reliable.

## Scope

**In scope (v1):**
- List of licenses with search
- Create, edit and delete a license entry
- Copy a serial to the clipboard
- Local, on-device storage

**Out of scope (for now):**
- iOS/iPadOS version
- Sync across devices
- Import from other password or license managers
- Distribution through the Mac App Store

## Architecture decisions

- Depth: personal tool. Moderate rigor: unit tests on model and logic, architecture kept simple.
- SwiftUI app generated with Tuist, Swift 6 strict concurrency, macOS 14 deployment target.
- Bundle identifier `it.stefer.SerialManager`, automatic signing.
- No ADR yet: the first non-trivial decision (storage) gets `docs/adr/0001-storage.md`.

## Milestones

1. Data model and local persistence for license entries
2. Main window: list, search, detail and edit form
3. Secure storage of serials and export/backup
